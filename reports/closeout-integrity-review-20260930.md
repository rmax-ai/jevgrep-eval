## Verified clean

- The local report is canonical JSON and its `report_digest` recomputes correctly. I compared every reported pilot cell's run ID, task/arm, status, evaluation success and named costs with its local sidecars; no mismatch appeared. Recomputed Jev totals are A0 `0.000`, A1 `0.720`, A3 `0.000`; the ledger's `0.768` exceeds pilot costs by the separately labeled `0.048` smoke entry.
- Hand-checked contrasting cells: `click-3764-a1-r0` timed out without evaluation and counts as failure; `click-3805-a3-r0` timed out despite a passing evaluation and counts as failure; `click-3805-a0-r0` passed with partial trace coverage and remains a task success while trace metrics are suppressed; `jinja-1852-a1-r0` and `click-3764-a0-r0` are admitted, completed, evaluated successes and correctly do **not** appear among exclusions. The six `jinja-1979`/`jinja-2061` arm cells are admission-rejected and excluded.
- All 10 admitted tasks form complete A0/A1/A3 triplets. Success counts are A0 4, A1 3, A3 4; paired estimates `-0.1` and `0.0` follow. With this all-eligible local set, primary and sensitivity comparisons coincide. The report withholds retrieval-only claims and avoids a superiority/equivalence conclusion; the null A3 contrast is visible.
- Tracked gold and hidden-test files are evaluator-side corpus artifacts (56 of each); `docs/leakage-controls.md` explains the workspace separation, and `materialize.py` filters evaluator paths from agent workspaces. The tracked identifier scan found sanitized trace-fixture placeholders and legitimate `key`/`token` vocabulary, not a credential in the tracked files inspected. Jevgrep MIT attribution and the BM25 upstream commit credit are present in `docs/provenance.md`.
- A disposable `env_failure` record with a passing leftover evaluation was excluded by `_cell`, confirming the primary status guard. Retained-byte SHA-256 values in the 36 local envelopes matched their corresponding envelope component fields.

## Residual uncertainty

- Static reading cannot prove the run host actually used the claimed model, launcher, network policy, provider rate or unmodified upstream source. The ignored raw runs are present locally but are outside the tracked handoff.
- No live provider receipt or independent credit history was available to establish actual cash charges. I did not replay agent executions or hidden tests, and did not run the repository's pytest suite.
- The sanitized fixture scan does not establish that every ignored/private runtime artifact is safe to publish; an evidence bundle needs its own hygiene pass before release.
